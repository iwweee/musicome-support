## English

### Musicome Privacy Policy
*Last updated: 2026*

#### 1. Data Controller
Musicome is developed and operated by independent developer Nico. For privacy requests or questions, contact: `codegem@hotmail.com`

#### 2. Data We Collect
- **No personal identifiers**: We do not collect, store, or upload your name, email, location, or any personally identifiable information.
- **Audio files**: Music you import is stored exclusively on your local device and never uploaded to any server.
- **Local preferences**: Playlists, play history, EQ settings, and preferences are kept strictly on your device.
- **Purchase data**: In-app transactions are handled entirely by Apple StoreKit. We have no access to your payment details.

#### 3. Online Metadata Retrieval
To enhance your listening experience, this app may query public online music databases for album artwork and lyrics using track titles and artist names:
- **No Personal Data Shared**: Requests only transmit public track titles and artist names. Your audio files are never uploaded, and no personally identifiable information (PII), unique device identifiers, or location data is ever collected or linked.
- **Local Caching**: Retrieved artwork and lyrics are cached strictly on your local device solely for playback display and are never used for profiling or tracking.

#### 4. Your Rights (GDPR)
If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, and port your data. As we collect no identifiable personal data, these rights are typically not applicable in practice. Contact `codegem@hotmail.com` for any data-related requests.

#### 5. Children's Privacy & Data Protection
Musicome is a general audience music player and is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13:
- **Zero Remote Data Collection**: The app operates entirely locally without user accounts or cloud databases. Audio files and preferences are stored strictly on your device. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
- **Local Data Erasure**: All local playback history and cached data can be permanently erased at any time simply by uninstalling the app from the device.
- **Contact & Inquiries**: If a parent or guardian has contacted us via email regarding their child, you may request the permanent deletion of correspondence records at any time by contacting `codegem@hotmail.com`.

#### 6. Policy Updates
We will notify you of significant changes to this policy through app version updates.
